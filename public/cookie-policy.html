Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, authenticate your login session, and analyze how you use the site.

Cookies may be set by the website you visit (first-party cookies) or by third-party services integrated into the website (third-party cookies).

5. How to Control Cookies

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Receive a notification before a cookie is set

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on
  • Meta Ads Opt-Out: Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Do Not Track (DNT): Enable in your browser settings (note: not all websites honor DNT signals)

5.3 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit Datoro, you will see a cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies (analytics & advertising)
  • Customize your preferences

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

6.1 Essential Cookies

If you disable essential cookies, certain features will not work:

  • You will not be able to log in or stay logged in
  • Your watchlists and preferences will not be saved
  • Security features may not function properly

6.2 Analytics Cookies

If you disable analytics cookies:

  • We will not be able to track usage patterns and improve the Service
  • You will still have full access to all features

6.3 Advertising Cookies

If you disable advertising cookies:

  • You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized
  • We will not be able to measure ad campaign effectiveness

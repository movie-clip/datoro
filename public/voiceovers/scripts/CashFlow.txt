Cash Flow

“Cash flow shows how much real money moves through a company — not accounting tricks, but actual cash in and out. Strong cash flow means the business can fund itself without begging investors.”

Free Cash Flow (FCF) Yield

“Free cash flow yield shows how much cash a company generates compared to its market value. High FCF yield means it’s producing strong returns — like dividends, but without paying them out.”

FCF Yield (Adjusted for SBC)

“Adjusted free cash flow yield removes stock-based compensation — the shares given to employees. It reveals how much true cash the business makes without hiding costs behind dilution.”

SBC Impact

“SBC impact shows how much stock-based pay reduces shareholder value. The bigger it is, the more your ownership gets diluted — it’s silent, but it hits your returns over time.”

Market Cap

“Market cap is the total value of a company’s shares — basically, what the market thinks the whole business is worth. Big cap means stability, small cap means potential and risk.”

P/E Ratio

“The P/E ratio compares price to earnings. High P/E means investors expect future growth; low P/E can mean the stock’s cheap — or that growth’s slowing down.”

Price to Sales (P/S)

“Price to sales shows how much investors pay for every dollar of revenue. Lower can signal undervalued, but for fast-growing tech, a high number isn’t always bad.”

Price to Book (P/B)

“Price to book compares a company’s market value to its net assets. Under one looks cheap — over three usually means investors expect strong returns or intangible value.”

EV to EBITDA

“EV to EBITDA measures a company’s total value against its core profits. It’s a cleaner version of P/E — great for comparing firms with different debt levels.”

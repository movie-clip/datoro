Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Datoro ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our financial data analysis platform at datoro.com (the "Service").

By using Datoro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you register for an account:

  • Email Address: Used for account authentication, communication, and security notifications
  • Name: Optional, used to personalize your experience
  • Password: Encrypted and stored securely using industry-standard bcrypt hashing

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Ticker searches, chart views, feature usage, session duration
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, IP address
  • Cookies: Authentication cookies (HttpOnly, Secure), session tracking, preferences
  • Analytics Data: Page views, click events, conversion events (via Google Analytics & Meta Pixel)

2.3 Financial Data

We do NOT collect or store your financial account information, brokerage credentials, or investment holdings. All financial data displayed on Datoro is sourced from third-party APIs (Financial Modeling Prep) and is publicly available market data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and manage your account, authenticate login sessions
  • Service Delivery: Provide financial data analysis, charts, AI insights, watchlists
  • Email Communications: Send verification emails, password resets, product updates, security alerts
  • Personalization: Remember your watchlists, preferences, and frequently viewed tickers
  • Analytics: Understand usage patterns, improve features, measure performance
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Compliance: Comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service
  • Marketing: Send promotional emails (you can opt-out anytime), retargeting ads

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

  • Render.com: Cloud hosting infrastructure (database, servers)
  • SendGrid: Transactional email delivery service
  • Financial Modeling Prep: Financial data API provider (receives ticker symbols only, no personal data)
  • Google Analytics: Website analytics and user behavior tracking
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Advertising and conversion tracking
  • Stripe: Payment processing for premium subscriptions (if applicable)

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

4.3 Business Transfers

If Datoro is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred to the new owner.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted over HTTPS with SSL/TLS certificates
  • Password Security: Bcrypt hashing with salt (cannot be reversed)
  • HttpOnly Cookies: Authentication cookies protected from JavaScript access (XSS prevention)
  • Database Security: PostgreSQL with row-level security, connection pooling, encrypted at rest
  • Rate Limiting: Protection against brute-force attacks and API abuse
  • Session Expiry: Automatic logout after 30 days of inactivity

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Opt-out of marketing communications and profiling
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for data processing at any time

6.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and share
  • Delete your personal data (with certain exceptions)
  • Opt-out of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: privacy@datoro.com

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication and core functionality (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics to understand user behavior (can be opted-out)
  • Advertising Cookies: Meta Pixel for retargeting ads (can be opted-out)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings or our Cookie Consent banner. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features from working.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Usage Logs: Retained for 90 days for analytics and debugging
  • Email Records: Retained for 1 year for compliance
  • Deleted Accounts: Permanently erased within 30 days (except as required by law)

9. Children's Privacy

Datoro is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using Datoro, you consent to the transfer of your data to these countries.

11. Third-Party Links

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

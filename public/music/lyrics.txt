🎶 Verse 1

It starts with one trade,
I don’t know why,
I checked P/E but forgot to buy.
I’m watchin’ revenue, margin, and growth,
To time the dip, but I never know both.

I tried so hard, for a clean DCF,
Got lost in notes that I over-kept.
All I know, at the end of the day,
Cash flow fades, and the price won’t stay.

🎶 Pre-Chorus

I kept the ratios tight,

🎶 Chorus
